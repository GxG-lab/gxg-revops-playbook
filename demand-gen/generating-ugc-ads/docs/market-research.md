# Market Research for UGC Concepts

Use this reference before proposing concepts. The purpose is to make the ad sound like a buyer's lived experience rather than a generic brand script.

## Research plan

1. Name the product category, audience, geography, key alternatives, and jobs-to-be-done.
2. Build 12 or more distinct searches across these groups:
   - Pain and trigger: `"{category}" "frustrated"`, `"{category}" problem`, `"{category}" "wish"`.
   - Skepticism: `"{category}" "worth it"`, `"{category}" scam`, `"{category}" regret`.
   - Alternatives: `"{competitor}" review`, `"{category}" alternative`, `"{category}" comparison`.
   - Outcomes: `"{category}" results`, `"{category}" routine`, `"{category}" before after`.
   - Communities and reviews: relevant public forums, app-store reviews, retailer reviews, Trustpilot, and independent category reviews.
3. Prefer primary customer posts and reviews. Record the URL, source type, date when visible, and a short supporting note.
4. Treat reviews and community posts as anecdotal. Do not convert them into prevalence or efficacy claims.

## Framework routing

| Situation in the research | Useful framework |
|---------------------------|------------------|
| Acute, recurring pain | PAS (Problem–Agitate–Solve) |
| Strong aspiration and clear contrast | BAB (Before–After–Bridge) |
| A compelling title or trigger | AIDA |
| Failed alternative plus recovery | Star–Story–Solution |
| High skepticism | Skeptic's arc / QUEST |
| Demonstrable product behavior | 4Ps (Promise–Picture–Proof–Push) |
| Common misconception | Myth-bust |

Never attribute a made-up phrase to customers. Adapt a finding into fresh copy when direct quotation is not available or appropriate.
