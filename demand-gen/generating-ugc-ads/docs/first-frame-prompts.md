# First-Frame Prompt Rules

The first frame anchors the creator, product, environment, and visual realism of a UGC ad. Write it as one dense prose prompt, 200–500 words for one creator/setting, followed by a short asset list.

## Required order

1. Camera: phone-facing or handheld treatment, angle, height, distance, and ratio.
2. Creator: approximate age range, relevant cultural context, posture, expression, and eye line.
3. Wardrobe: concrete, plausible garments and one realistic imperfection.
4. Setting and props: lived-in, audience-appropriate details rather than a generic studio.
5. Product: exact placement and interaction state, consistent with supplied reference assets.
6. Lighting: source, direction, color-temperature mix, and natural shadow behavior.
7. Texture and camera artifacts: fabric, skin texture, surface wear, subtle motion or phone-camera imperfection.

## Realism checks

Use four or five of these only where they fit the target audience:

- Asymmetry, worn items, organic clutter, and non-perfect framing.
- Regionally and culturally plausible props rather than generic labels.
- Mixed or imperfect lighting and natural color variation.
- Phone-camera details such as a slightly imperfect white balance or a little motion blur.

Avoid visible text overlays, watermarks, app UI, and product claims rendered into the image. Do not specify traits that stereotype or misrepresent a demographic.

## Prompt shape

```text
9:16 vertical phone-camera still. {camera and framing}. {creator, expression,
wardrobe}. {specific setting and 5–10 plausible details}. The supplied {product}
is {placement}; it is visible but not distorted or relabeled. {light and texture}.
{realism details and restrained camera artifacts}. No text, watermark, overlay, or UI.
```
